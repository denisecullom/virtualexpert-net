The AI Client Playbook + Kit

A step-by-step guide to landing clients with AI, plus the done-for-you prompts, scripts and follow-up sequences to run it. Written for freelancers, VAs, consultants and coaches who'd rather do the work than chase it.

Most AI advice stops at “ask ChatGPT to write a cold email.” This guide gives you the whole sales process around it: who to target, how to start conversations, how to follow up, and how to turn calls into signed clients. It's the same pipeline discipline I used to grow a company from $5M to $16M, sized for a business of one.
