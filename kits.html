Questions

Before you buy

Do I need to be technical?

No. If you can set up a Google Drive folder and copy and paste, you can install these. Every step is written out for both Claude and ChatGPT, and you practice on your own account first.

Whose account do the agents live in?

The client's. They pay for their own Claude or ChatGPT plan (about $20 a month), so they own the setup and their data never sits in your account. You get invited in, never sent a password.

Can I resell the kits or give them to clients?

You can install the setups for as many clients as you like, and give each client the filled-in client guide. Please don't resell or share the kit PDFs themselves.

Are the suggested fees guaranteed?

No. They're starting points to help you price, not market rates. The kits show you how to price from the client's own savings, which is the number that matters.

How is this different from 20 AI Agents That Pay for Themselves?

The book teaches 20 agents for your own business. The kits package agents into services you install for clients, with the selling, intake, testing, handover and care steps a client project needs. A few agents appear in both, adapted for client work here.
