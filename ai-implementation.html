Choosing the right solution

Not every process needs the same kind of AI.

One of the most common mistakes I see is buying the most advanced tool for a job that needs something simpler, or the reverse. I help you match each workflow to one of three levels.

Level 1

Human-in-the-loop

AI drafts, researches and suggests. A person reviews and approves before anything goes out.

Best for: client communication, proposals, content, hiring and anything where judgment or relationships matter.

Level 2

Full automation (RPA)

Rule-based automations run repetitive steps end to end: moving data between systems, filling forms, sending notices.

Best for: high-volume, predictable tasks with clear rules, like invoicing, data entry, scheduling and reporting.

Level 3

AI-intelligent workflows

AI agents read context, make decisions within limits you set, and handle exceptions, escalating to a person when needed.

Best for: triage, customer support, lead qualification and work that varies too much for fixed rules.

Most companies end up with a mix. The right choice depends on risk, volume, how much the work varies, and how ready your team and data are.

Some, yes, especially workflows and agents built on tools like Claude and ChatGPT. For custom development or larger automation projects, I introduce you to specialized teams and oversee the work on your behalf.

What size companies do you work with?

Small and mid-sized businesses get the most out of this, because a clear plan and good adoption pay off quickly when there is no in-house AI team.

How do you keep our data safe?

Data and privacy are part of every recommendation: which tools are approved, what information can go into them, and where a person must stay in the loop.
