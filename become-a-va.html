Questions

Frequently asked

Do you take a cut of what I earn?

No. Companies pay a one-time placement fee when they hire. You keep everything your clients pay you.

Is a job guaranteed once I'm listed?

No. I match VAs to the requests that come in, so how often you're introduced depends on demand for your skills, hours and rate.

Do I need a certification?

No, but you do need to show real, everyday use of AI in your work. A short example is often more convincing than a certificate.
