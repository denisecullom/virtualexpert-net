Your free prompts

10 AI prompts to land your next client

Fill in the [brackets], paste into your AI assistant, and edit the result so it sounds like you. Bookmark this page, or save it as a PDF.

  1. 01

    Find your best-fit client

    Find

    I'm a [your role] who helps [type of client] with [result]. My three favorite past clients were: [describe each in one line]. Based on what they have in common, describe my ideal client in 5 bullet points: industry, size, role of the decision maker, the problem that makes them look for help, and where they spend time online.

  2. 02

    Sharpen your offer into one sentence

    Find

    Here is how I describe what I do today: [paste]. Rewrite it as one plain sentence a busy prospect would understand in 5 seconds, using the format "I help [who] [get result] without [pain]." Give me 5 versions, from most conservative to boldest.

  3. 03

    Find where your clients gather

    Find

    My ideal client is [paste from prompt 1]. List 10 specific places they spend time online or in person where I could start real conversations: named communities, groups, newsletters, podcasts, events or hashtags. For each, give one sentence on how to show up there without being salesy.

  4. 04

    Teach AI your voice

    Reach

    Here are 3 messages or posts I've written that sound like me: [paste]. Describe my writing style in 6 short rules (tone, sentence length, words I use, words I avoid). From now on, write everything for me following these rules.

  5. 05

    Write a personal first message

    Reach

    Here is a prospect's recent post or website section: [paste]. I help [who] with [result]. Write a message under 80 words that mentions one specific detail from what I pasted, connects it to a problem I solve, and ends with a low-pressure question. No flattery, no exclamation marks. Follow my voice rules.

  6. 06

    Reconnect with past clients and contacts

    Reach

    Write a short, warm check-in message to a former client or contact named [name] I worked with on [project] about [time] ago. Mention something specific about our work together, share one thing I'm doing now ([new offer]), and ask if anyone they know might need help with it. Under 70 words.

  7. 07

    Follow up without being pushy

    Follow up

    I sent this message [days] days ago and got no reply: [paste]. Write 3 follow-ups to send over the next 2 weeks. Each should add something useful (a tip, a resource, a quick observation about their business), not just "bumping this up." Keep each under 60 words.

  8. 08

    Prep for a discovery call in 5 minutes

    Close

    I have a discovery call with [name], [role] at [company]. Here is their website/LinkedIn summary: [paste]. Give me: 3 likely problems they're facing, 5 open-ended questions to ask, and 2 objections they might raise with a calm, honest response to each.

  9. 09

    Draft a proposal fast

    Close

    Here are my notes from a call with a prospect: [paste]. Write a one-page proposal with: their situation in their own words, the result they want, my recommended approach in 3 steps, timeline, investment of [price], and a clear next step. Plain language, no jargon.

  10. 10

    Plan your weekly pipeline hour

    Close

    Every week I can spend [minutes] on getting clients. Build me a simple weekly routine that covers new outreach, follow-ups, and staying visible, with a target number for each. Then give me a 4-column tracker layout (name, last contact, next step, date) I can paste into a spreadsheet.
