Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

This policy explains what information VirtualExpert.net collects and how it is used.

What I collect

  • Details you submit in a form, such as your name, email address and message.
  • Basic usage data, such as pages visited, the site you came from and your general location. This comes from the hosting provider and from Google Analytics, which uses cookies to count visits. It doesn't tell me who you are.

How I use it

  • To reply to your message or request.
  • To send emails you signed up for, such as launch notices or weekly tips. Every email includes a way to unsubscribe.
  • To deliver products you buy.

I don't sell your information. Form submissions are processed by Netlify, which hosts this site. Purchases are handled by a third-party checkout provider under its own privacy policy. Site analytics are provided by Google under Google's privacy policy; you can block analytics cookies in your browser or with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

Your choices

You can ask me to see, correct or delete the information I hold about you at any time through the contact page.
