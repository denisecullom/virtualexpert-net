What every agent looks like

Savings math · Agent 1: Inbox Triage

Before: 45 minutes a day reading, sorting and answering routine email. After: 15 minutes reviewing the summary and sending drafts. 30 minutes × 20 workdays = 10 hours a month × $53 = $530/month, at an executive assistant's median pay plus benefits.

Scheduled task · Weekdays, 7:10am

“Triage my inbox and draft routine replies. Read emails received in the last 24 hours. Sort into Needs me today, Can wait, Ignore. Flag anything about money, contracts or deadlines. Draft replies to routine emails. Never send an email or delete anything.”
