How it works

From request to hire in four steps.

  1. Tell me what you need

    Fill in the short request form below: the work, the hours, your tools and your budget. It's free.

  2. Get a shortlist

    Within five business days I send you two or three matched VAs, with their experience, rates and the AI tools they use.

  3. Interview and choose

Questions

Frequently asked

Who employs the VA?

You do, or you contract with them directly. I make the match; the working relationship, the rate and the contract are between you and your VA.

When do I pay the placement fee?

Only after you decide to hire. I send you a secure Stripe payment link for the one-time $400 fee, and I introduce you to your VA to start.

What if it doesn't work out?

If your VA leaves or isn't the right fit within the first 60 days, I'll find you a replacement at no extra charge.

Can I hire more than one VA?

Yes. The placement fee applies to each VA you hire.
