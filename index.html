Questions

Frequently asked

Will AI replace virtual assistants?

AI replaces tasks, not the person who knows the client's business, checks the work and keeps things running. VAs who set up and run AI for their clients become more valuable. The ones who only sell manual hours are the ones at risk.

Do I need to be technical?

No. If you can use email and a paid AI assistant like ChatGPT or Claude, you can do everything here. Every agent is built from plain-English instructions you copy and adjust. No coding.

Is it safe to use AI with my clients' data?

It can be, if you follow a few rules: get written permission, use the client's own business account, never paste sensitive data into free tools, and keep a person approving anything that goes to customers or moves money. The free guide has the full checklist.

What can I charge for AI services?

Price AI work as a setup fee plus a monthly fee to run it, not by the hour. The free guide gives starting price ranges for 10 services and a simple formula for setting your own.

I'm a freelancer or consultant, not a VA. Is this for me?

Yes. The AI agents and the client-getting Playbook work for any service business. If your main goal is landing more clients, start with the Playbook + Kit or the free pipeline scorecard.

What is the AI-Ready VA course?

An 8-module course with templates, a toolkit and a certification you can show clients. It covers finding the work AI should take over, building workflows, keeping client data safe, and pricing and selling it all. See the course.
