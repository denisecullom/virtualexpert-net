The system

Four steps from “who do I reach out to?” to signed client.

The same pipeline thinking I used to run sales teams, scaled down for a business of one and sped up with AI.

  1. Find

    Define the client who pays well and stays, then use AI to build a focused list of real prospects.

  2. Reach

    Write short, personal messages in your own voice, at a pace you can keep up every week.

  3. Follow up

    Most yeses come after the third touch. Use simple sequences so no lead goes cold.

  4. Close

    Prep calls, write proposals and handle objections faster, with AI doing the first draft.

Questions

Frequently asked

Do I need to be technical to use this?

No. If you can use email and a free AI tool like ChatGPT or Claude, you can use everything here. Every prompt is copy-and-paste.

Won't AI outreach feel spammy?

Only if you use it that way. The whole approach is about fewer, better messages: AI does the research and first draft, and you add the human touch before anything goes out.

Which AI tools do I need?

A free or paid account with any major AI assistant is enough. The prompts are written to work across tools.

I'm brand new. Is this too advanced?

No. The guide starts with picking who you serve and what you offer, so it works whether you're landing your first client or your fiftieth.

What if I need help applying it to my business?

That's what the Client Pipeline Audit is for. I'll look at your specific offer and outreach and tell you exactly what to change.
