Questions VAs ask

        

          

            What makes a good AI prompt for client work?
            
A good prompt tells the AI who it's working for, what the business is like, the exact task, the rules it must follow and the format you want back. Rules matter most for client work: tell it never to invent prices, dates or facts, and to ask you questions instead of guessing.

          

          

            Does this work with ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini?
            
Yes. The prompts are plain text, so you can paste them into any AI assistant. For work you repeat every week, save the prompt as the instructions in a project, so you only paste the new input each time.

          

          

            Is my client's information sent anywhere?
            
No. The generator runs entirely in your browser and nothing you type is sent or stored. Before you paste real client data into any AI tool, check your client has agreed, use a paid plan, and leave out passwords, card numbers and health details.

          

          

            Can I edit the prompt after I copy it?
            
Yes, and you should. Treat it as a strong first draft. Add a real example of the client's writing or a past report, and the results get much better.
